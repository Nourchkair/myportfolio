How to read the metrics

  • Pitch variation is the standard deviation of voiced F0 in semitones. Higher usually means more intonation movement, not necessarily better taste.
  • Micro pitch is the mean frame-to-frame F0 change in semitones. It is a closer proxy for moment-to-moment liveliness than overall range.
  • Energy variation is the standard deviation of speech-frame level in dB. Higher means more dynamic emphasis and stress.
  • Pause is the share of the clip below the adaptive speech threshold. WPM is words per minute of voiced speech.
  • Centroid is the spectral centre of gravity. Lower is darker/warmer; higher is brighter/more forward.
  • First audio is measured from prompt send to the first PCM chunk. Chunks shows stream granularity.

What this page cannot tell you

  • Whether a voice sounds male, female, young, old, trustworthy or warm. Listen and judge.
  • How the voice feels over a five-minute conversation, after interruptions, on a phone or over Bluetooth.
  • Whether 2.5 Native remains available. It is a preview model; do not switch production without approval.
  • Exact production prompt equality: the current system prompt here is reconstructed from source, not read from the live token.

Provisional root-cause ranking

  • 1. Voice choice. Iapetus is one of the flattest voices in this matrix. Sadachbia and Zubenelgenubi move more in pitch and energy.
  • 2. Opening prompt. The current tour prompt tells Nupa to mention “live, interactive, personalized”, “interrupt”, “navigate” and “stop at any time”. The transcripts reproduce that list almost verbatim. The short/refined opening removes it.
  • 3. Model character. 2.5 Native is consistently more expressive than 3.8 Live, but its first audio is 2.8–4.0s slower in like-for-like tests and it is a preview model with an uncertain retirement path.
  • 4. Prompt density. The live system prompt is ~30k characters and ~30 rules. The current prompt makes openings longer and less dynamic, but it is not the only cause.
  • 5. Playback. App-level Iapetus samples retain the same pitch variation as direct Gemini PCM. The frontend is not the primary source of the robotic timbre, though it adds latency and fragility on jittery/Bluetooth paths.

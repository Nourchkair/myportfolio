2. What we collect and why

Portfolio browsing

The portfolio works without an account. It stores a local visual environment preference in your browser. The static site does not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or a third-party analytics product. Cloudflare, as the hosting and security provider, may process standard technical data such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, and security events under its terms.

Access and session ownership

Nupa does not require an account or access key. Your browser keeps a server-issued random owner token and a session identifier in localStorage; they are hashed server-side where used for ownership checks. The token is a bearer credential: clearing browser storage or using another browser creates a new anonymous identity. Rate limits and spending caps protect the service.

Conversations and AI processing

When you use Nupa, we process the text you enter, voice transcripts, assistant replies, conversation summaries, language, the portfolio section/project/flow/chapter you are viewing, navigation actions, inferred visitor context (such as whether you appear to be a recruiter, business owner, or technical visitor, and your stated interests), model/token usage, estimated cost, error data, timestamps, and the accepted eligibility/terms/privacy consent version and timestamp. We do not store microphone audio files; Live audio is streamed to Google Gemini for real-time processing, and transcripts may be stored as part of the conversation.

Inquiries, consent, and appointments

When an inquiry or appointment workflow is enabled and you choose to use it, we may collect your name, email, optional phone number, organization, role, language, timezone, inquiry summary, requested service, optional budget/timeline, consent choices and evidence, requested appointment time, booking status, Calendar event identifier, and Google Meet link. External CRM/Calendar writes are disabled unless explicitly enabled.

Email delivery

Resend is currently disabled. If transactional email is enabled, we would process the recipient address, message type, subject and body needed for delivery/retry, provider message identifier, delivery status, attempts, and bounce/complaint events. We do not use email for marketing.

Administration and security

The private administrator signs in with Google OAuth. We process the allowlisted Google account email/name, a signed admin session cookie, and audit records for administrative actions. The administrator can access conversations, CRM records, email job content and metadata, cost data, and audit/operational logs for support, quality, security, and operation of the service.

Cost and operational records

We keep pseudonymous request identifiers, model names, token counts, estimated costs, rate-limit counters, text and Live spending reservations, active voice-session leases, and error/operational events to enforce spending caps, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and keep the service reliable.

External links

LinkedIn and GitHub links open third-party sites. We do not send your data to them unless you click a link; their own privacy policies apply.

3. AI processing and Google Gemini

Nupa uses Google Gemini for text and real-time voice processing. Your prompts, responses, and Live audio/transcripts are sent to Google for processing. The browser receives only a short-lived Live token; the permanent Gemini API key stays server-side. Google processes this data under its Gemini API Additional Terms of Service (effective 23 March 2026), the Google APIs Terms of Service, the Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy, and Google's privacy terms.

Nupa's production Gemini API project is linked to an active Google Cloud Billing account and uses Google's paid Gemini API services. Under Google's Paid Services terms, including its Data Processing Addendum, Google processes prompts, responses, and Live audio/transcripts to provide the service and states that it does not use them to improve its products. Google may still log and retain data for a limited period to detect prohibited use, maintain security, and comply with legal or regulatory obligations. Google's independent retention and security practices are governed by its terms; we cannot promise zero retention, complete confidentiality, or absolute privacy.

Conversations processed before the project moved to paid Gemini API services were processed under the then-applicable unpaid-service terms. This policy does not retroactively claim paid-tier protections for those earlier conversations.

Google's terms require users of the Gemini API and API clients to be 18 or older and prohibit API clients directed towards or likely to be accessed by under-18 users. Nupa is a public beta for visitors aged 18 and older; rate limits and spending caps apply. Paid Services are now active. We do not control Google's independent retention, security, or abuse-monitoring practices.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to train our own generalized AI models. Nupa's own opinions and conclusions are not Nour's documented beliefs. AI outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unexpected; they are not legal, financial, medical, or other professional advice.

6. Retention

  • Conversation sessions, messages, summaries, and visitor context: 7 days after the last activity by default; expired sessions are cleaned up on a scheduled run. You may request earlier deletion.
  • Voice audio: not stored as audio files by us; Google's processing is governed by its terms.
  • Contacts, inquiries, consent records, appointments, and the CRM Sheet: as long as needed to handle the inquiry/appointment and for legitimate business or legal purposes. They can be deleted on request unless retention is required by law or necessary for a dispute.
  • Email jobs: non-queued email content and delivery metadata are deleted after 30 days; queued/sending jobs remain until processed. Webhook events are deleted after 30 days. Resend is currently disabled.
  • Operational/error logs: up to 30 days.
  • Cost/usage ledger, text and Live reservation records, active voice-session leases, admin audit, and idempotency records: retained longer where needed for spending controls, accounting, security, duplicate prevention, or legal obligations. These records contain pseudonymous identifiers, not conversation text.
  • Backups: no automated backup schedule is currently active. If encrypted backups are enabled, they are stored in a private Google Drive folder and retained under the then-current backup schedule.

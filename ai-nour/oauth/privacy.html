AI Nour / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Prepared for review 26 November 2026

Review copy. This page is not published or linked from the portfolio until the OAuth/legal review is approved.

AI Nour is an artificial-intelligence representation of Nour Chkair. It is not Nour Chkair. This policy explains what information is processed when you use AI Nour, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

Controller: Nour Chkair, Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. Privacy contact: nourchkair@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • Conversation content: text messages, voice transcripts, language preference, and the portfolio section you are viewing when you interact with AI Nour.
  • Contact and inquiry details: name, email, phone (optional), organization, role, inquiry summary, requested service, budget range, timeline, and consent choices.
  • Appointment details: requested time, timezone, booking status, calendar event identifier, and Google Meet link.
  • Email delivery data: recipient address, message type, delivery status, provider message identifier, retry count, and bounce/complaint events.
  • Technical and usage data: session identifiers, owner/session tokens stored in your browser, rate-limit counters, model/token usage, estimated cost, error and audit logs.
  • Google user data from resources you explicitly authorize: CRM spreadsheet records, calendar availability/events, app-created Drive files and encrypted backups, and Gmail drafts if that optional path is enabled.

Why we process it

  • To answer questions about Nour's public professional portfolio.
  • To record and respond to inquiries, manage consent, and prepare appointments.
  • To create, reschedule, or cancel calendar events and unique Google Meet links you request.
  • To protect the service: authentication, rate limits, duplicate prevention, spending caps, abuse prevention, and audit logging.
  • To improve reliability and evaluate whether the prototype meets its requirements.

AI processing and third parties

AI Nour uses Google Gemini for text and real-time voice processing. Conversations may be processed by Google under its API terms. Do not share sensitive personal, financial, health, or confidential information. The service also uses Cloudflare (hosting, database, security), Google Workspace APIs (Sheets, Calendar, Drive, and optional Gmail drafts), and Resend for approved transactional email. Resend processes recipient addresses and delivery metadata to send acknowledgements, confirmations, reminders, reschedules, and cancellations.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or use Google user data to train generalized AI models. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above, consistent with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention

  • Conversation sessions and messages: up to 7 days by default, unless a shorter period is configured.
  • Inquiries, consent records, and appointment records: as long as needed to handle the inquiry and meet legal/operational obligations.
  • Email delivery metadata: up to 30 days; message bodies only as long as needed for retry and diagnostics.
  • Usage/cost and security logs: up to 30 days unless needed for an investigation.
  • Encrypted backups: retained according to the approved backup schedule; deleted when no longer needed.

Your choices and rights

You can ask for access, correction, or deletion of your information, withdraw consent, or ask how your data is processed by emailing nourchkair@gmail.com. You can use the portfolio without the AI companion; the reading view is assistant-free. You can revoke Google access from your Google Account permissions page.

Cookies and local storage

The prototype uses browser storage for an owner token, a session identifier, an access key for the isolated prototype, and a language preference. It does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Security

Access is protected server-side. Permanent API credentials remain on the server; browsers receive short-lived voice tokens. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so avoid sharing sensitive information in conversation.

Children

AI Nour is intended for professional visitors and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

This draft will be updated before production launch. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
